3. Activities and inherent risks
Movement practice involves exertion, balance, changes of direction, floor work and proximity to other people. Risks include slips, trips, falls, collisions, accidental contact, fatigue, dizziness, strains, sprains, bruises and other injuries, which may include fractures, concussion, serious injury, permanent disability or death. Capoeira may include kicks, sweeps, evasions, cartwheels, handstands, inversions, acrobatics and close interaction in the roda. Salsa may include turns, quick footwork, partnered movement and rotating partners. Samba may include repetitive footwork, pivots, changes of direction, vigorous exertion and footwear that affects balance or traction. Mobility Flow and Yoga may include loaded joint positions, stretching, low transitions and controlled inversions. Instruments, equipment, floors and footwear or bare feet can affect grip, balance and safety. These examples are not exhaustive. Other current or future practices may involve different movements, exertion, partner contact, equipment or instruments. Participants should ask about the specific activity and its risks before joining; these examples do not describe every future practice.

5. Health, instructions and participation responsibilities
I am responsible for considering whether I can safely participate and for seeking appropriate medical advice if I have a health concern. The Studio and its instructors do not provide medical diagnosis or treatment. I will not participate when I believe it is unsafe. I will disclose relevant participation limitations privately to the instructor, follow reasonable safety instructions, stay within my skill level, use suitable clothing and footwear, respect the space and other participants, and stop and notify the instructor if I feel unwell, dizzy or injured. I do not need to disclose a detailed medical history on this form. The Studio or instructor may pause or end participation when behavior, a health concern or failure to follow safety instructions creates a safety risk. This does not change the participant’s separate payment or refund terms.

Punctuality and courtesy: Arrive on time and ready to participate. If you expect to be late, notify the Studio or instructor. Check with the instructor before joining a class already in progress; a missed warm-up or safety briefing may make it unsafe to join.

Health and safety: Stay home if you are unwell or may expose others to a contagious illness. Follow posted health and safety guidance, wear clothing and footwear suitable for the practice, and bring water. Tell the instructor privately about relevant injuries or limitations, and stop if you feel unwell, dizzy or injured.

Cancellations and refunds: Notify the Studio or the program provider as soon as possible if you cannot attend. Cancellation deadlines, refunds, credits and rescheduling follow the terms provided for your specific booking, membership or program. This waiver does not establish a new cancellation deadline or change those terms.

Instructor or schedule changes: Keep your contact information current so the Studio or program provider can reach you about instructor substitutions, cancellations or rescheduling. Check any class updates before attending. Applicable refund or credit terms remain those of your booking or program.

Respect for the space and personal belongings: Respect instructors, fellow participants and the studio space. Keep belongings clear of the practice area and take them with you when you leave. Avoid bringing valuables; the Studio does not provide secure storage. Any release of property-loss claims remains limited by section 6.

Reporting hazards: If I notice an unsafe condition, damaged equipment or another significant hazard, I will stop the affected activity and notify the instructor or studio manager. For a child, I will also share any significant concern about the child's readiness to participate.

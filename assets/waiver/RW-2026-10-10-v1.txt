3. Activities and inherent risks
Movement practice involves exertion, balance, changes of direction, floor work and proximity to other people. Risks include slips, trips, falls, collisions, accidental contact, fatigue, dizziness, strains, sprains, bruises and other injuries, which may include fractures, concussion, serious injury, permanent disability or death. Capoeira may include kicks, sweeps, evasions, cartwheels, handstands, inversions, acrobatics and close interaction in the roda. Salsa may include turns, quick footwork, partnered movement and rotating partners. Mobility Flow and Yoga may include loaded joint positions, stretching, low transitions and controlled inversions. Instruments, equipment, floors and footwear or bare feet can affect grip, balance and safety. These examples are not exhaustive. Other current or future practices may involve different movements, exertion, partner contact, equipment or instruments. Participants should ask about the specific activity and its risks before joining; these examples do not describe every future practice.

4. Assumption of inherent risks
I understand that inherent risks can cause injury or loss even when reasonable care is used. I knowingly and voluntarily choose to participate and assume the inherent risks of the covered activities. This assumption does not waive any claim that California law does not allow to be waived.

I am responsible for considering whether I can safely participate and for seeking appropriate medical advice if I have a health concern. The Studio and its instructors do not provide medical diagnosis or treatment. I will not participate when I believe it is unsafe. I will disclose relevant participation limitations privately to the instructor, follow reasonable safety instructions, stay within my skill level, use suitable clothing and footwear, respect the space and other participants, and stop and notify the instructor if I feel unwell, dizzy or injured. I do not need to disclose a detailed medical history on this form.

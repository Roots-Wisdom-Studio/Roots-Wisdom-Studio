2. Voluntary participation and personal boundaries

3. Activities and inherent risks
Movement involves exertion, balance, floor work and proximity to others. Risks include slips, falls, collisions, fatigue, dizziness, strains, sprains and other injuries, including fractures, concussion, permanent disability or death. Capoeira may involve kicks, sweeps, inversions, acrobatics and close interaction in the roda. Salsa may involve turns and partner work; Samba, repetitive footwork and vigorous exertion; Mobility Flow and Yoga, stretching, loaded joints and low transitions. Floors, footwear, equipment and instruments affect safety. These examples are not exhaustive; future practices may have different risks.

4. Assumption of inherent risks
I understand that inherent risks can cause injury or loss even when reasonable care is used. I knowingly and voluntarily choose to participate and assume the inherent risks of the covered activities. This assumption does not waive any claim that California law does not allow to be waived.

5. Health, instructions and participation responsibilities
I will consider whether I can safely participate and seek medical advice when needed. The Studio does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment. I will share relevant limitations privately with the instructor; no medical history is required on this form. The Studio or instructor may pause or end participation for safety reasons.

Punctuality and courtesy: Arrive on time. Notify the instructor if late and check before joining a class in progress. Respect instructors, participants and the space.

Health and safety: Stay home when unwell or contagious. Follow safety guidance, stay within your skill level, use suitable clothing and footwear, and bring water. Stop and tell the instructor if unwell, dizzy or injured.

Hazards and belongings: Stop the affected activity and report unsafe conditions or damaged equipment. Keep belongings clear of practice areas and take them when leaving. The Studio does not provide secure storage; property-loss claims remain subject to section 6.

Cancellations and changes: Notify the Studio or provider promptly if unable to attend. Keep contact details current and check class updates. Cancellation, refund, credit and rescheduling terms remain those provided for the booking or program; this waiver does not change them.

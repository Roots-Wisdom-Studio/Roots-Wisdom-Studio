PLEASE READ CAREFULLY. This agreement includes an assumption of risk and a limited release of legal claims, including certain claims based on ordinary negligence. Ask questions before signing.

1. Participants and covered activities

This agreement covers the named participant's participation in Capoeira offered by Roots Legacy Ventures LLC dba Roots & Wisdom Studio (the Studio), directly or jointly with Uriel E. Arauz dba Sitio Alegre Capoeira; studio-run Salsa Socials; and other practices, classes, workshops or events the Studio operates in the future. It also covers use of studio-controlled practice and common areas, floors and equipment when arriving, attending and leaving. It applies to members, new and current students, drop-ins and free trials.

  • Independent programs: Independently run Salsa classes, Samba and guest workshops have their own providers' registration, instruction, payment, cancellation and waiver arrangements. For those programs, this studio waiver covers the Released Parties' own premises, services and conduct; it does not replace the provider's agreement or automatically release the provider. A class taking place here does not by itself make it a studio-run program.
  • Other practices: Movement, dance, mobility, yoga, martial arts, music and cultural activities may take place at the Studio. The examples here are not a complete list of future practices. Ask about each activity's risks; materially different activities may require a supplemental waiver. Public sidewalks, parking areas outside the Studio's control, off-site activities, independent rentals and childcare are outside this agreement.

2. Voluntary participation and personal boundaries

Participation is voluntary. I may decline a movement, partner interaction, physical contact or hands-on adjustment, or pause or stop at any time. I will tell the instructor when I need a modification or do not want contact. Instructors cannot guarantee an activity is suitable for me.

3. Activities and inherent risks

Movement involves exertion, balance, floor work and proximity to others. Risks include slips, falls, collisions, fatigue, dizziness, strains, sprains and other injuries, including fractures, concussion, permanent disability or death. Capoeira may involve kicks, sweeps, inversions, acrobatics and close interaction in the roda. Salsa may involve turns and partner work; Samba, repetitive footwork and vigorous exertion; Mobility Flow and Yoga, stretching, loaded joints and low transitions. Floors, footwear, equipment and instruments affect safety. These examples are not exhaustive; future practices may have different risks.

4. Assumption of inherent risks

I understand that inherent risks can cause injury or loss even when reasonable care is used. I knowingly and voluntarily choose to participate and assume the inherent risks of the covered activities. This assumption does not waive any claim that California law does not allow to be waived.

5. Health, instructions and participation responsibilities

I will consider whether I can safely participate and seek medical advice when needed. The Studio does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment. I will share relevant limitations privately with the instructor; no medical history is required on this form. The Studio or instructor may pause or end participation for safety reasons.

  • Punctuality and courtesy: Arrive on time. Notify the instructor if late and check before joining a class in progress. Respect instructors, participants and the space.
  • Health and safety: Stay home when unwell or contagious. Follow safety guidance, stay within your skill level, use suitable clothing and footwear, and bring water. Stop and tell the instructor if unwell, dizzy or injured.
  • Hazards and belongings: Stop the affected activity and report unsafe conditions or damaged equipment. Keep belongings clear of practice areas and take them when leaving. The Studio does not provide secure storage; property-loss claims remain subject to section 6.
  • Cancellations and changes: Notify the Studio or provider promptly if unable to attend. Keep contact details current and check class updates. Cancellation, refund, credit and rescheduling terms remain those provided for the booking or program; this waiver does not change them.

This release covers claims for injury, illness, death or property loss arising from covered participation or use of studio-controlled areas, floors or equipment, only to the extent based on a Released Party's ordinary negligence and permitted by California law. Examples include ordinary negligence in instruction, supervision, inspection or maintenance. It does not automatically release independent Salsa or Samba providers or their businesses.

  • THIS RELEASE DOES NOT COVER GROSS NEGLIGENCE, FRAUD, WILLFUL INJURY OR MISCONDUCT, VIOLATION OF LAW, OR ANY CLAIM OR LIABILITY THAT CANNOT LAWFULLY BE WAIVED.

7. Parent or legal guardian consent for a minor

For a participant under 18, I confirm I am the named child's parent or legal guardian with authority to sign. I understand the activities and risks, consent to participation, and will explain safety expectations and share relevant limitations. To the extent California law permits, I agree to the assumption of risk and limited release on the child's behalf and release my own claims on the same limited basis. Claims that cannot lawfully be waived remain unaffected. Each child has a separate signed record. Each adult, including a spouse or partner, signs for themselves. A child turning 18 must sign their own adult waiver before further participation.

8. Emergency assistance

I authorize the Studio to contact the emergency contact and call emergency services when reasonably necessary. For a minor, staff may seek emergency medical evaluation if the guardian cannot promptly be reached, subject to applicable law. This does not make staff medical providers or guarantee a particular response. Outside medical charges remain subject to the provider's terms and applicable law.

9. Duration, updates and existing agreements

This agreement applies from signing to future covered participation until replaced or withdrawn in writing to hello@rootsandwisdomstudio.com. Withdrawal does not undo earlier covered participation. A new version or supplemental waiver may be needed after material changes in activities, operating entity or location. It does not release claims for earlier incidents or change membership, payment or cancellation terms. California law governs. If one provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions apply to the extent permitted by law.

10. Optional photo and video permission

The Studio takes photos and videos for its social media, website, marketing, promotion and educational materials. I choose Yes or No; either choice allows attendance. Yes authorizes use of the participant's image and captured voice without payment or advance approval of each use. No, or a missing choice, gives no permission. For a child, the guardian chooses.

  • If choosing No, I will also tell the instructor or manager before class; the Studio will honor No even if I forget. I may change my choice by emailing hello@rootsandwisdomstudio.com. Withdrawal stops new uses after a reasonable processing period; copies already shared by others may remain. Permission is for the Studio, not an independent provider's own promotion, sale to unrelated third parties, or publication of medical or emergency-contact details.

11. Acknowledgment and electronic signing

I have read and understand this agreement, including the limited release, and have had an opportunity to ask questions. I sign voluntarily and consent to electronic records and signing. My typed full legal name and checked agreement box are my electronic signature. For a child, I sign as the parent or legal guardian. I can save or print my copy; paper signing is also available.
